AkihabaraAnime, manga, retro games, figures and electronics. Try a multi-floor arcade such as GiGO or Taito Station, and a used-games store like Super Potato. Many shops open at 10:00 or 11:00. Lunch here: curry, ramen or a themed cafe.See photos

~17:15
To OdaibatravelWalk 10 minutes to Shimbashi, then the Yurikamome elevated line, a scenic ride across the Rainbow Bridge. Get off at Daiba, the closest stop to the fountain. Sunset is about 16:45, so the lights are coming on.
~17:45-18:45
Tokyo Aqua Symphony fountain & Rainbow BridgeThe giant fountain show at Odaiba Seaside Park (up to 150 m high, with music and colour lights), free, with the lit Rainbow Bridge and Tokyo Tower behind it. A 10-minute show runs every hour on the hour from 11:00 to 21:00, except 16:00. Watch the 18:00 show, and the 19:00 one if you want a second look. Best view is from the deck by the Statue of Liberty replica, about 3 minutes from Daiba Station.See photos
~19:00
Unicorn Gundam & DiverCityfoodThe life-size Gundam statue in front of DiverCity Tokyo Plaza lights up and moves at set times in the evening. Dinner in the mall.See photos
~20:45
Back to UenotravelYurikamome to Shimbashi, then the Ginza Line to Ueno, about 50 minutes.
The "waterfall" is the Tokyo Aqua Symphony fountain (opened March 2026). Check the show schedule on the day, since shows can pause for wind or events, and the Gundam show times.
